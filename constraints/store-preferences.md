# Store Preferences — Rosharon, TX

## Primary Store: HEB
- Location: Rosharon/Alvin/Pearland area
- Best for: Fresh produce, meats, dairy, bakery, pantry staples
- Specialty: HEB brand products, Hispanic/Mexican ingredients, Texas regional items
- Shopping frequency: Weekly

## Bulk Store: Costco
- Location: Pearland or League City
- Best for: Bulk proteins, bulk grains/rice/beans, large format dairy, bulk snacks
- Shopping frequency: Every 2-3 weeks
- Note: Buy family-size portions that can be frozen or stored

## Supplementary Store: Kroger
- Location: Alvin/Pearland area
- Best for: Specialty items not at HEB, Kroger brand alternatives, sale items
- Shopping frequency: As needed
- Note: Check weekly digital coupons

## General Shopping Strategy
- Plan primary shopping trip at HEB each week (Saturday morning)
- Costco run every 2-3 weeks for bulk restocking
- Kroger only for specific items or when HEB is out of stock
